One project, start to finish

How the dispatch planner went from a morning spreadsheet to a tested plan.

Problema product that decays

A radiopharmaceutical used in hospital imaging loses half its activity in under two hours. Each morning, planners decided in a spreadsheet which vehicle serves which hospital, when it leaves, and how much to produce.

Framingone number connects it all

Routes, fleet size and dose all hang on the departure time. Once the departure is fixed, decay is a formula, and one hard problem becomes many ordinary routing problems.

Methodsweep, route, back-solve

The planner solves a routing problem (OR-Tools) for every departure time and fleet size, keeps the plans under the production ceiling, and back-solves each stop's dose from the chosen departure.

How I knewtests, then the manufacturer's numbers

Physics tests on the decay maths, a pinned reference day, and production figures that reproduce the manufacturer's workbook. It is now in acceptance testing with a company within the Kalbe group.
