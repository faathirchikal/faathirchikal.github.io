## Now

I lead the data team at Kalbe Farma while two planners I built go through acceptance testing. I'm
looking for a Senior or Lead applied data science role in optimisation and decision science.

## How I work

**I lead a data team.** We run two-week sprints. I take requests from the head of department and
business users across the group, set priorities, consult on the approach, and give the final review
before an analysis reaches its users. Each intern pairs with a full-time team member. I also coach
data scientists in other companies of the group.

**I build decision engines.** Most of my work turns a daily operational decision into something a
solver or a model can answer: routes, stock levels, which message to send, which product to show.
Before building, I settle how I'll know it works. For a solver that means a reference day and the
planners' own numbers. For a model it means a held-out period and a baseline. For an LLM agent it
means an answer key written by the analysts who own the numbers. The
[essay]({{ "/essay/" | relative_url }}) has the long version.

**I ship end-to-end products.** The two planners (built with a small team) and the agent platform are multi-user web applications
with logins, roles, a database and a CI pipeline. The recommender and the news monitor run as
scheduled jobs that feed live systems, as the campaign allocator did until 2026. I write much of the code with AI coding
agents (Claude Code), under code review and automated tests.

## Background

I studied mathematics at Universitas Indonesia and wrote my thesis on bandits for changing
environments, which was [published in 2021](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/1722/1/012096). I
taught probability and statistics as an assistant lecturer. At Blibli.com I started as a data analyst on
sales reporting, forecasting and ad hoc analysis, then moved to the product catalogue and computer vision. I joined Kalbe Farma in 2023 as a data scientist.
Alongside that job I completed a Monash Master of Data Science, with a
[thesis on multi-agent reinforcement learning]({{ "/work/jakarta-signal-control/" | relative_url }})
for traffic signals in Jakarta.
